[Groovy] Class PasskeyService

    • grails.plugins.passwordless.PasskeyService 
    • class PasskeyService
extends java.lang.Object

      A user's passkeys: listing and removing them. Passkeys belong to the account, and are stored by Spring Security under its email. Telling people when one is added is the application's, on PasskeyRegisteredEvent.
